You're in a ML interview at Meta

The interviewer asks :

"How do you choose a vector database?"

You reply :
"I'd benchmark recall and latency."

That's only 20% of the problem.

Here's how you break it down 🧵:
A vector database isn't just a search algorithm, it's a production data system. You should evaluate it like a database, not just a library.

The wrong choice will crush you with operational costs, not slow queries.
Here's the 3 point decision framework :

Filtering Needs: Do you need to filter on metadata before the vector search (pre-filtering) or after (post-filtering)?

Pre-filtering is harder for many DBs but essential for production RAG.
Update Patterns: How often does your data change?

High-volume writes and deletes are handled very differently by systems like Milvus vs. simpler ones like FAISS.
Operational Model:
Managed service (Pinecone, Zilliz) vs. Self-hosted (Qdrant, Weaviate)?

The "cheaper" open-source option can cost 10x more in DevOps time.
The difference in mindset:

Junior: "I'll pick the one with the best HNSW implementation and benchmarks."

Senior: "I'll ask about the metadata schema, the required update rate before I even look at a benchmark. An index that can't filter properly is useless in production."
Pro tip :

Mention the billion-scale cost problem.

"For massive scale, I'd investigate if the DB supports disk-based indexes. Keeping a billion vectors in RAM is financially impossible for most companies, so I'd prioritize solutions that gracefully handle memory/disk trade-offs."
